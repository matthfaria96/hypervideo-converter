No FFmpeg code is included.

AI Upscale engine
-----------------
upscayl-bin (Contents/MacOS/upscayl-bin), from Upscayl's upscayl-ncnn, release 20251207-174704.
License: GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (upscayl-LICENSE-AGPL-3.0.txt in this folder).
Source code: https://github.com/upscayl/upscayl-ncnn
It runs as a separate program; HyperVideo Converter starts it and reads its output files.
It builds on Real-ESRGAN (Copyright (c) 2021, Xintao Wang, BSD 3-Clause License,
https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) and Tencent's ncnn (BSD 3-Clause License).

  Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ ;
  model page: https://openmodeldb.info/models/4x-HFA2k . Converted to ncnn format by Upscayl.
Models whose licenses forbid commercial use (Remacri, UltraSharp, Ultramix) are not included.
