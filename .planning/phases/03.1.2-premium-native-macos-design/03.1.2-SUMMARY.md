## Design system
- `Theme.swift`: semantic colors, `Spacing` (8 pt grid), `Radius`, `card(_:)` prominence styles.
- `Motion.swift`: quick / standard / emphasized / snappy springs, row, content, banner and pop transitions, `hoverLift`; every curve becomes a 0.12 s crossfade with Reduce Motion.
- `Compat.swift`: glass styles and soft scroll edges on macOS 26+, bordered/material fallbacks on 15.

## Motion on every action
Page switch, empty ↔ list, rows added/removed/reordered, selection, inspector swap, tab switch, crop mode, profile card choice (with symbol replace), estimate numbers (numeric text), progress bars, Convert button title, finished banner (bounce), drop target (scale + bounce), hover on cards and row buttons.
