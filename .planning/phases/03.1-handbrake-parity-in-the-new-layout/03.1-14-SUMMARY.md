## Screenshots
Window-only captures (`screencapture -o -x -l <CGWindowID>`, id from a CGWindowList helper), 1278x732. The sample clip was generated with ffmpeg (6 s, 1280x720 with black bars, two AAC tracks en/por, two mov_text subtitle tracks, three chapters). Files are in `screenshots/`: tab-summary, tab-dimensions, tab-filters, tab-video, tab-audio, tab-subtitles, tab-chapters, tab-tags, inspector-all-videos (two videos, none selected: "Editando: todos os 2 videos").

## Deviations (Rule 1 - visual defects found in screenshots)
1. Subtitle track rows were wider than the 460 pt column, which widened the window and clipped content. Controls are now split over two rows and the name field is flexible (SubtitleTrackRow.swift).
2. pt-BR "First Track" / "All Tracks" were too long for the segmented picker and pushed the Audio and Subtitles panels past 460 pt. They are now "Primeira" / "Todas".
3. The crop label "Esquerda" wrapped to "Esquerd a". The label column is wider and single-line (CropSection.swift).

## Known remaining cosmetic items
- Video and Dimensions panels still measure about 470 px at the left edge. Nothing is clipped.
- The Tags card runs close to the right edge of the window.
- The Tags title field is empty for the sample, because the container title is not read from ffmpeg-written MP4 metadata. This was not investigated.
- The hint card for multi-selection is below the fold in `inspector-all-videos.png`; the header "Editando: todos os 2 videos" is visible.
