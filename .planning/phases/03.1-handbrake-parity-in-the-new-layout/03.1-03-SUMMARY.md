## Findings (probes)
- **Pixel aspect:** `AVAssetTrack.naturalSize` of a PAR-tagged track is the DISPLAY size (480x360 at 4:3 reports 640x360). `GeometryPlanner` therefore treats naturalSize as display size: `.none` output stays square-pixel display size; `.automatic` divides the width by the PAR and tags the PAR on the output (480x360 + 4:3). Covered by `naturalSizeOfTaggedClipIsDisplaySize`.
- **Edit lists:** `AVAssetReaderTrackOutput` sample PTS are media time, so a late first video frame (leading empty edit) is invisible to a sample probe. `AVAlignment` uses the first non-empty edit segment of the video track instead of a reader probe (plan suggested a reader; changed after the probe returned 0).
- **MaximizePowerEfficiency=false** is accepted by the writer for `.quality`; kept.
- **Framerate above source rate:** AVFoundation's composition output does not duplicate frames when the target rate exceeds the source rate (30 fps source to 60 gives 30 frames on the grid). Downsampling, peak and variable work as specified. The "every FrameRate encodes" test therefore uses a 120 fps source so each rate is reachable.
